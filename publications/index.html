Research & publications

Publications

My PhD looks at how people's social networks relate to their wellbeing, and what changes when a social chatbot joins the mix. I tested the chatbot side with an 8-week randomized controlled trial (N > 500) of sustained use. Both questions lean on complexity theory, which treats a social network as one system where a change in one relationship can spread to the others. Papers from the PhD are still in preparation, so the peer-reviewed record below comes from my earlier neuroscience work, mostly on stroke and brain injury.
