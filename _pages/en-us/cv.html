Résumé

Leo Huang

I study how people make decisions, then design and test the products and processes they move through. I'm a behavioral scientist with more than six years of designing and running large-scale experiments and turning behavioral and telemetry data into product decisions, including UX research roles at CIBC and Slack. My social psychology PhD at the University of Toronto is on human–AI interaction, with a focus on social chatbots, wellbeing and social networks. Causal inference and predictive modeling are where my expertise runs deepest.
